# B NOVA SEO

Prepare concrete, white-hat SEO agreements and estimates for B NOVA. The document must explain what the client pays for without promising positions, traffic, applications or revenue.

## B NOVA Requisites

- Use current B NOVA requisites from `bnova-contract/references/requisites.md` when preparing contracts, acts, estimates or signature blocks.
- Current contact defaults: `8-983-009-35-32`, `info@bnova.site`, `@E_Berest`, `https://bnova.site/`.

## Contract Profile – Pension «Vera»

- Use the prior website-development contract number to continue numbering. After `2026/07-01`, use `2026/07-02` for the SEO agreement dated 30 July 2026 unless the user provides another number/date.
- State payment only by period: month 1 is preparation – `40 000 ₽` plus SEO – `80 000 ₽`, total payment for the first month `120 000 ₽`; months 2–6 are `80 000 ₽` per month. Do not show a six-month cumulative price unless the user explicitly asks.
- State `НДС не облагается.` only when the user confirms the Executor works without VAT. The customer’s tax regime does not define the wording.
- Keep the current `.рф` domain as the primary domain. Do not include `.ru` registration, migration or redirects unless the user explicitly commissions them.
- Exclude ad budget, paid placements/links, stock or production costs, paid services and work beyond the agreed development/content scope. Do not list unrelated roles such as doctors or lawyers as excluded services.
- Do not include artificial behavioural-factor manipulation, automated clicks, doorway pages, hidden text or other search-engine-rule violations.

## Scope and Intake

- In month 1, include baseline visibility and positions, technical audit, competitor analysis, semantic core and clustering, page map, analytics/goals verification, key on-page optimisation and the first report.
- In months 2–6, describe the work in full sentences: page structure and copy, service-page updates within the agreed limit, trust blocks, metadata, FAQ, linking, conversion paths, indexation control, local cards, query/CTR analysis, user-behaviour analysis, regular semantic expansion and final audit/report. Expand descriptions, not the promised page count.
- Ask for detailed services, conditions, prices, proof documents and materials before the respective pages are published. A high-level service list is sufficient before signing.
- Treat public 2GIS/Yandex reviews as a source for reputation and response work. Ask for card links and the person responsible for answering new reviews; do not require a separate permission merely to check an open card.
- Do not ask for CMS, hosting, DNS or domain access if the user confirms they already hold it. Remove discovery questions that are already decided.

## B NOVA Contract Layout

- Create an editable `.docx` and visually verify it with the document renderer.
- Use A4 portrait, Arial 10.5 pt body, `#2456D6` headings, a small B NOVA header, muted footer with page number, pale-blue price bands, light-gray table rules and a two-column requisites/signature block.
- Put the detailed six-month plan, estimate and exclusions in Appendix 1. Include an act template and only the intake questions still needed for the project.
- Use short en dash `–` only. Never use the long em dash character `U+2014` (длинное тире) in the document, source copy, metadata or skills.

## Acceptance Check

- The contract has the correct number and date.
- The first-month and recurring monthly prices are unambiguous; no cumulative total appears unless requested.
- Every month has concrete SEO actions, but no ranking or lead guarantee.
- No long em dash appears.
- Re-render every page and fix clipping, split payment tables and orphaned month headings before delivery.
